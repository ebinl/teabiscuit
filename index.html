THE MIND BEHIND TEA BISCUIT

TEJAS VIJAY

Founder & Director

“The journey begins with a dream, a tea shop and a young mind that started working at 18.”

Every journey has a story, and Tejas Vijay’s began with a passion for creativity and the determination to build something of his own.

Starting his professional career at just 18, Tejas began as a freelancer, working without an office or a dedicated workspace. A nearby tea shop became his everyday workplace, where he would sit with his laptop, work on projects and turn his ideas into reality.

That humble beginning became the inspiration behind the name Tea Biscuit Media — a reminder that meaningful things can grow from the simplest beginnings.

A JOURNEY ACROSS INDUSTRIES

Over the years, Tejas developed his expertise in design, branding, photography and visual storytelling, working with businesses across diverse industries, including travel agencies, schools, hospitals, colleges, real estate agencies, restaurants and companies serving clients across India.

One of his notable milestones was contributing to the Sustainable Coastal Karnataka magazine by the Karnataka State Travel Operators Association (KSTOA), personally capturing its photographs and handling its visual design and layout.

THE VISION BEHIND TEA BISCUIT

What started as freelance creative work gradually evolved into a bigger vision — building a company that brings creativity, digital solutions and technology together under one roof. Today, Tea Biscuit Media brings together:

  • Creative & Branding: Brand identity, graphic design and visual communication.
  • Media Production: Photography, videography and content creation.
  • Digital Marketing: Social media management and digital growth strategies.
  • Technology: Digital solutions, web development and technology-driven services.

For Tejas, this is more than a business. It is a reflection of his journey, the experiences that shaped him and his belief that every brand deserves the right creative, digital and technological support to grow. From a tea shop to building a multi-dimensional creative venture. The journey continues.
